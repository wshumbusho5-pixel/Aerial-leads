How fast can you buy my house in Columbus?

We can close in as few as 7 days after the offer is accepted. If you need more time, 30, 60, or 90 days, that works too. You choose the date.

Will you make a fair offer?

Offers are based on current Franklin County market data and actual repair costs. We provide a written breakdown. You keep what you would otherwise lose to agent commissions, closing costs, repairs, and months of carrying costs.

What condition do you buy in?

Any condition. Fire damage, water damage, mold, foundation issues, deferred maintenance, full of belongings. We do not require a single repair before closing.

Are there any fees or hidden costs?

None. We pay all closing costs. No agent commissions, no processing fees. The offer amount is the net amount received by the seller.

Where in Columbus do you buy?

Throughout all of Franklin County. Columbus, Westerville, Dublin, Grove City, Reynoldsburg, Whitehall, Gahanna, Upper Arlington, Hilliard, Worthington, and every neighborhood in between.
