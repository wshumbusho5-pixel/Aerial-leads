Willy Shumbusho

The Day That Changed Everything

Growing up, I never thought we'd lose our home. But life doesn't care about your plans.

My father fell behind on property taxes. A few payments became a year. The penalties compounded. The letters didn't stop. We were drowning, and nobody reached out a hand.

We lost our home. My father lost everything he'd built. And not one person offered to help.

I watched my father — a proud man — be broken by a system that only saw a debt, never a family. I never forgot what that felt like.

The Promise I Made

I made myself a promise: if I ever had the means, I would be the person who shows up when no one else does.

In 2022, I put that promise into action. Not as a faceless corporation. Not chasing volume. Because I remember — clearly — what it felt like to have no options and no one in your corner.

If you're facing what my family faced — you're not alone anymore. Tax problems. Inherited property. Foreclosure. Whatever your situation, I'm here.

What Drives Me Every Day

The moment someone realizes there's a way out — that look on their face — that's why I do this. Not the deals. The people.

"Every person I help is the family I wish someone had helped when we needed it most."

— Willy Shumbusho
