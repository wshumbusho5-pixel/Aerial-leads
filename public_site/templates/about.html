About Lifeline Home Buyers

A Property Acquisition Firm Built on Experience.

Lifeline Home Buyers is a privately funded residential acquisition company headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, operating under Orteza Group. We acquire properties directly from homeowners across Franklin County and Central Ohio.

Willy Shumbusho

Founder & Managing Director

Leadership

Founder oversight. Structured operations. Direct accountability.

Willy Shumbusho is the founder of Orteza Group, a Columbus-based holding company. Lifeline Home Buyers operates as the real estate acquisition division, founded in 2022 after Willy experienced firsthand what happens when a family faces property loss with no guidance and no options.

Today, he provides strategic oversight on acquisition operations and capital deployment across Central Ohio. Our acquisitions team handles property evaluations, title coordination, and seller communication under his direct supervision.

Lifeline operates with structured underwriting, internal capital reserves, and dedicated title coordination. Every transaction closes through licensed title professionals with full transparency on pricing.

Why This Company Exists

This business started because of a house my family lost.

In 2012, my father fell behind on property taxes. The letters kept coming. Penalties, interest, fees. No one reached out to explain what the options were. We lost the house.

Lifeline exists so that does not happen to other families. I built this company to provide what my family never received: clarity, structure, and a real offer on the table.

Every property we evaluate, every offer we write, every closing we coordinate is informed by that experience. This is not a side project. It is the company I built to solve a problem I lived through.

How We Operate

Our Acquisition Process

Step 01
Initial Review
Submit your property address. Our team reviews public records, tax status, title history, and comparable sales within 24 hours.
Step 02
Valuation & Offer
Written cash offer with a transparent breakdown. No hidden fees, no obligations. Review on your timeline.
Step 03
Title Coordination
Our team handles title search, lien resolution, and closing logistics through licensed title professionals.
Step 04
Closing
Close in as few as 7 days or on your schedule. Back taxes, liens, and payoffs handled at the table.
Capabilities

What Lifeline Handles

Private Capital

Internal reserves and private capital relationships. No bank loans, no mortgage approvals. Funds available at closing regardless of property condition.

As-Is Acquisitions

We purchase properties in any condition. No repair requests, no renegotiation after walkthrough. The offer we present is the offer we close on.

Title Resolution

Back taxes, liens, and title defects are resolved at closing. We coordinate directly with the title company on behalf of the seller.

Flexible Timelines

Close in 7 days or 90 days. Leaseback arrangements available. Schedule built around the seller, not around us.
