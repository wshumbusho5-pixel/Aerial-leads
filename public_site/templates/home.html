Our Approach

Our Acquisition Model

Lifeline evaluates each property individually. Depending on the situation, transactions may be structured as a direct purchase, a partnership acquisition, or a portfolio addition. This flexibility allows us to move on properties that traditional buyers and lenders cannot.

We specialize in properties with complicating factors: title issues, deferred maintenance, tax delinquency, probate, code violations, or occupancy situations. These are the deals most buyers walk away from. Lifeline is structured to handle them.

Transaction Structure

How Transactions Are Funded

Transactions are structured through internal reserves and private capital relationships. This eliminates lender delays, appraisal contingencies, and financing fall-through, the most common reasons transactions collapse.

Private Capital

Internal reserves and private capital relationships. No bank loans. No mortgage approvals. Funds available at closing regardless of property condition.

No Inspection Contingencies

We purchase as-is. No repair requests, no renegotiation after walkthrough. The offer we present is the offer we close on.

Clear Title Handling

Back taxes, liens, and title defects are resolved at closing. We coordinate directly with the title company.

Flexible Timelines

Close in 7 days or 90 days. Leaseback arrangements available. Schedule built around the seller.

FAQ

Common Questions About Our Process

How do I verify Lifeline is a legitimate company?

Lifeline Home Buyers is a real estate investment company based in Columbus, Ohio. Our acquisitions team evaluates every property, and our founder maintains direct oversight on strategy and operations. All transactions close through licensed title companies.

How are offers calculated?

Offers are based on comparable sales, property condition, and current market data. We provide a written breakdown showing how the number was determined. There is no obligation to accept.

What condition does the property need to be in?

Any condition. We acquire properties with deferred maintenance, code violations, fire damage, hoarding situations, and occupied or vacant status. No repairs or cleanout required.

What about outstanding mortgages or liens?

Mortgages, tax liens, and other encumbrances are paid from sale proceeds at closing. If the property is underwater, we communicate that before proceeding.

What is the typical closing timeline?

7 days with clear title. 14 to 30 days is standard. Extended timelines of 60 to 90 days are available. Leaseback arrangements can be structured if additional time is needed.

Are there fees or commissions?

No. Lifeline covers all closing costs. No agent commissions, no processing fees. The offer amount is the net amount received by the seller.
