Why this company exists

I know what happens when no one shows up.

In 2012, my father fell behind on property taxes. Not by a little — by enough that the letters didn't stop coming. Seventeen letters over three months. Each one with a new number attached to it: penalties, interest, fees I didn't understand.

We lost the house. My father lost everything he had spent years building. And in all that time, not one person reached out to explain what the options were. No buyer. No attorney. Nobody.

I started Lifeline because I know exactly what that silence feels like. When someone calls me with back taxes or an inherited property they don't know what to do with — I'm not looking at a spreadsheet. I'm thinking about my father.

That's not a marketing line. That's just what actually happened.

Common questions

The things people actually want to know.

Is this a scam? How do I know you're real?

Reasonable question. I'm a private buyer based in Columbus — not a franchise, not a call center. I'm the one who picks up the phone, comes to see the property, and signs the contract. You can verify me, look up the address after closing, and talk to me before committing to anything.

Are you going to lowball me?

My offers are below retail value — that's honest. I buy as-is and close fast, and that has a cost. But I'll show you exactly how I arrived at the number, and you can compare it to whatever else you have on the table. No pressure to accept.

What happens to everything inside the house?

Take what you want, leave the rest. I've bought houses full of furniture, full of junk, and full of things families weren't ready to sort through. None of that is your problem to fix before we close.

What if I still owe on the mortgage?

That's fine. The mortgage gets paid off at closing from the sale proceeds, same as any normal sale. What's left over goes to you. If the amount owed is close to or more than the property's value, we'll have that conversation honestly.

How fast can this actually close?

Seven days is possible if the title is clean. Most closings happen in 14–30 days. If you need more time — 60 days, 90 days — that's fine too. I work around your timeline.

Do I pay anything?

No fees, no commissions, no closing costs on your end. What I offer is what you walk away with.
