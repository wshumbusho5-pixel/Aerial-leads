What Happens Next

The Tax Delinquency Timeline in Ohio

In Ohio, delinquent property taxes do not remain static. Penalties compound quarterly, and the county has multiple enforcement mechanisms. Understanding the timeline helps homeowners make informed decisions.

  • Tax Lien Sale: Franklin County can sell the lien on your property to a third party investor
  • Tax Foreclosure: If taxes remain unpaid, the county can initiate foreclosure proceedings and take ownership
  • Compounding Penalties: Interest and fees accelerate every quarter the balance remains outstanding
  • Credit Impact: Tax liens become public record and affect credit standing
  • Legal Costs: The longer the delinquency persists, the more expensive any resolution becomes

Lifeline provides an alternative. We acquire the property, resolve all back taxes at closing, and the seller receives net proceeds. The debt is cleared. The property transfers. It is done.
