Common Questions About Selling to Cash Buyers

How do cash home buyers actually work?

We purchase homes directly from homeowners using our own funds — no banks involved. We typically pay below retail market value, but you avoid repairs, agent commissions (6%), closing costs, and months of waiting. For many sellers, the net is comparable or better.

Is selling to a cash buyer the right move for me?

It depends on your situation. Cash buyers are the right fit when you need speed, when the property needs significant repairs, when you’re facing foreclosure or a tax sale, or when you simply want a guaranteed sale without the uncertainty of the open market.

How much will Lifeline pay for my house?

Every property is different. We typically offer 70–85% of after-repair market value, depending on condition, location, and current market data. We show you exactly how the number was calculated — nothing is hidden.

What if I get an offer and decide not to accept?

You walk away with no obligation whatsoever. No pressure calls. No follow-up harassment. If the offer doesn’t work for you, just say so and we part ways.
