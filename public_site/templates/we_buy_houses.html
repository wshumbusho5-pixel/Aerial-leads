Common Questions About Selling to Cash Buyers

How do "we buy houses" companies work?

We purchase homes directly from homeowners for cash, typically below market value but without the need for repairs, agent commissions, or lengthy waiting periods. We make our profit by renovating and reselling or renting the properties.

Is selling to a cash buyer right for me?

Cash buyers are ideal if you need to sell quickly, your house needs repairs you can't afford, you're facing foreclosure or tax sale, or you simply want a hassle-free sale without showings and negotiations.

How much will you pay for my house?

Every property is different. We typically offer 70-85% of after-repair market value, depending on condition and location. While less than retail, you save 6-10% in agent fees, plus repair costs and months of carrying costs.

Are there any obligations if I get an offer?

Absolutely none. Our offers are free and no-obligation. If it doesn't work for you, just say no. No pressure, no follow-up calls if you're not interested.
