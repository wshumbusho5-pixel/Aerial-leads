FAQ

Common Questions About Selling to Cash Buyers

How do cash home buyers work?

We purchase homes directly from homeowners using private capital. No banks involved. We typically pay below retail market value, but you avoid repairs, agent commissions (6%), closing costs, and months of waiting. For many sellers, the net is comparable or better.

Is selling to a cash buyer the right move for me?

Cash buyers are the right fit when you need speed, when the property needs significant repairs, when you are facing foreclosure or a tax sale, or when you want a guaranteed sale without the uncertainty of the open market.

How much will Lifeline pay for my house?

Every property is different. Offers are based on comparable sales, property condition, and current market data. We provide a written breakdown showing how the number was determined. There is no obligation to accept.

What if I decide not to accept the offer?

You walk away with no obligation. No follow-up pressure. If the offer does not work for you, we part ways.
