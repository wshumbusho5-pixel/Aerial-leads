# Lifeline Home Buyers
## Virtual Assistant Interview Guide

---

# PART 1: INTERVIEW QUESTIONS
*Questions to ask the candidate*

---

## Welcome & Rapport (2-3 minutes)

*"Thanks for taking the time to meet with me today. I'm excited to learn more about you. Before we dive in, how's your day going so far?"*

---

## Section A: About You

**1. I'd love to hear your story** - what led you to pursue virtual assistant work, and what excites you most about this opportunity?

**2. Walk me through a typical workday for you** - how do you structure your time when working remotely?

---

## Section B: Experience & Skills

**3. Have you had any exposure to real estate, sales, or customer service?** Even if it's not direct experience, I'd love to hear about transferable skills you bring to the table.

**4. Our work involves speaking with homeowners going through challenging times** - foreclosure, inherited properties, financial hardship. How do you approach conversations that require empathy and professionalism?

**5. Tell me about a time you turned a "no" into a "yes"** - or at least kept the door open for future conversation.

---

## Section C: Problem-Solving Scenarios

**6. Scenario:** *You call a homeowner and they say, "I've had 10 investors call me this week. Why should I talk to you?"*

**How would you respond?**

*Good answer looks like: Shows empathy, differentiates Lifeline, doesn't pressure*

**7. Scenario:** *A lead seemed interested last week but now won't return your calls. What's your follow-up strategy?*

*Good answer looks like: Persistent but respectful, tries different contact methods, knows when to move on*

---

## Section D: Work Environment & Availability

**8. Tell me about your workspace setup** - do you have a quiet environment, reliable internet, and the tools needed for calls and data entry?

**9. What hours are you available to work?** And how flexible can you be if we need coverage during US business hours (Eastern Time)?

---

## Section E: Motivation & Fit

**10. What does "going above and beyond" look like to you?** Can you share an example?

**11. Where do you see yourself in a year?** I want to make sure this role aligns with your goals.

---

## Closing

**12. What questions do you have for me?** I want to make sure you have a clear picture of what working with Lifeline Home Buyers looks like.

---

### Wrap-up Script:
*"Thank you so much for your time today. I really enjoyed our conversation. Here's what happens next: I'll review all candidates and get back to you within [X days]. Do you have any final questions before we wrap up?"*

# PART 2: ANSWERS TO CANDIDATE QUESTIONS
*What to say when they ask you questions*

---

## "What is Lifeline Home Buyers?"

**Answer:**
"Lifeline Home Buyers is a real estate solutions company based in Ohio. We help homeowners who need to sell their properties quickly - whether they're facing foreclosure, inherited a property, dealing with divorce, or simply need a fast, hassle-free sale.

The name 'Lifeline' comes from a personal place. I watched my family lose our home to tax delinquency when I was younger, and no one was there to help. I started this company so I could be the lifeline that wasn't there for my family - helping homeowners find solutions before it's too late.

We're not just another investor looking to make a quick buck. We genuinely care about helping people through difficult situations."

---

## "What exactly will I be doing?"

**Answer:**
"Your main responsibilities will include:

1. **Cold Calling** - Reaching out to homeowners from our lead lists to see if they're interested in selling
2. **Lead Qualification** - Having conversations to understand their situation and motivation
3. **CRM Management** - Logging all calls, updating lead statuses, and scheduling follow-ups
4. **Appointment Setting** - Booking qualified leads for follow-up calls with me
5. **Follow-up Calls** - Reaching back out to leads who weren't ready initially

You'll be the first point of contact for most homeowners, so you're really the face of Lifeline Home Buyers."

---

## "What are your expectations for this role?"

**Answer:**
"Here's what success looks like in this role:

**Daily Expectations:**
- Make 80-150 calls per day (depending on list quality)
- Have 10-20 meaningful conversations
- Log every call in our CRM system
- Submit an end-of-day report

**Quality Expectations:**
- Be professional and empathetic on every call
- Follow our call scripts while keeping conversations natural
- Accurately document lead information
- Flag hot leads immediately

**Communication:**
- Be responsive during work hours
- Ask questions when you're unsure
- Give me honest feedback about what's working and what's not

I'm not looking for perfection - I'm looking for someone who shows up consistently, cares about the work, and is always improving."

---

## "What kind of contract/employment is this?"

**Answer:**
"This is an **independent contractor** position, not full-time employment. Here's what that means:

- **Payment:** You'll be paid [hourly rate OR per lead OR base + commission - specify your structure]
- **Schedule:** Flexible, but we'll agree on set hours for consistency
- **Taxes:** You're responsible for your own taxes as a contractor
- **Equipment:** You'll need your own computer, headset, and internet connection
- **Trial Period:** We'll start with a [1-2 week] trial period to make sure it's a good fit for both of us

I provide all the tools, training, leads, and scripts. You provide the talent and dedication."

## "What training will I receive?"

**Answer:**
"You won't be thrown into the deep end. Here's our training process:

**Week 1:**
- Company overview and our mission
- Understanding distressed homeowners and their situations
- CRM and tools training
- Call scripts and role-playing
- Shadow calls (you listen while I call)

**Week 2:**
- Supervised live calls (you call while I listen)
- Real-time feedback and coaching
- Gradually increasing independence

**Ongoing:**
- Weekly team check-ins
- Call reviews and feedback
- Updated scripts and strategies
- Open door for questions anytime

I want you to feel confident before you're on your own."

---

## "What tools will I use?"

**Answer:**
"We use a simple tech stack:

1. **VA Portal** - Our custom dashboard where you'll see your assigned leads, log calls, and track progress
2. **Browser Dialer** - Make calls directly from your web browser (no phone needed)
3. **Google Workspace** - For email and calendar
4. **Communication** - [WhatsApp/Slack/etc.] for quick team communication

Everything is web-based, so you just need a computer with Chrome and a good internet connection."

---

## "What are the working hours?"

**Answer:**
"The ideal calling hours for reaching homeowners in Ohio are:

- **9 AM - 12 PM Eastern Time** (morning)
- **4 PM - 7 PM Eastern Time** (evening)

I'm flexible on exact scheduling, but I need at least [X hours] per day during these windows. We can discuss what works best for your timezone.

The most important thing is consistency - homeowners are more likely to answer during these times."

---

## "Is there room for growth?"

**Answer:**
"Absolutely. Here's how I see the growth path:

1. **VA (Current Role)** - Cold calling and lead qualification
2. **Senior VA** - Handle warm leads, more complex conversations, help train new VAs
3. **Acquisitions Manager** - Negotiate deals directly with homeowners
4. **Team Lead** - Manage a team of VAs

I'm building this company for the long term, and I want people who grow with me. If you prove yourself reliable and skilled, there's definitely opportunity to take on more responsibility and earn more."

---

## "Why should I work for Lifeline instead of another company?"

**Answer:**
"Great question. Here's what makes Lifeline different:

1. **Mission-Driven** - We're not just chasing deals. We genuinely help people in tough situations.

2. **Respect** - I treat my team like partners, not disposable workers. Your input matters.

3. **Tools & Training** - I invest in proper systems so you can do your job well.

4. **Communication** - I'm accessible. If you have a problem, we solve it together.

5. **Growth** - I want you to grow. If you're good, I'll give you more opportunities.

I can't promise it'll always be easy, but I can promise I'll always be fair and supportive."

---

## "When can I start?"

**Answer:**
"If we both feel it's a good fit after this interview, here's the timeline:

1. **Decision** - I'll let you know within [2-3 days]
2. **Onboarding** - Complete contractor agreement and setup (1-2 days)
3. **Training** - [1 week] of training
4. **Go Live** - Start making calls with supervision

So realistically, you could be making your first calls within [2 weeks]."
