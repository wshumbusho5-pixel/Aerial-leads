## 2. SERVICES

The Contractor agrees to perform the following services ("Services"):

### For Property Acquisition Specialist (PAS):
- Contact property owners via phone regarding potential property sales
- Follow Company-provided scripts and communication guidelines
- Log all calls and outcomes in the Company's CRM system (AERIAL)
- Set appointments for the acquisitions team
- Submit daily performance reports
- Maintain professional communication standards

### For Investor Development Specialist (IDS):
- Research and identify potential real estate investors
- Conduct outreach via phone and email to build the buyer network
- Collect and document investor criteria and contact information
- Maintain accurate records in the Company's database
- Submit daily performance reports
- Follow up with interested investors as directed

## 4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

### 4.1 Relationship
The Contractor is an **independent contractor** and not an employee of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture.

### 4.2 No Employee Benefits
The Contractor is not entitled to any employee benefits including but not limited to:
- Health insurance
- Retirement benefits
- Paid vacation or sick leave
- Workers' compensation
- Unemployment insurance

### 4.3 Taxes
The Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes arising from compensation received under this Agreement, including but not limited to income taxes and self-employment taxes. The Company will not withhold any taxes from payments to the Contractor.

### 4.4 Direction and Oversight
The Contractor agrees to perform Services under the direction and oversight of the Company. The Company shall provide scripts, training, quality standards, and work schedules that the Contractor is expected to follow.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

### 6.1 Confidential Information
The Contractor acknowledges that during the engagement, they will have access to confidential and proprietary information including but not limited to:

- Lead data and contact information
- Property owner information
- Investor/buyer lists and criteria
- Company scripts, training materials, and methodologies
- The AERIAL platform and its features
- Business strategies and processes
- Financial information

### 6.2 Non-Disclosure
The Contractor agrees to:
- Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party
- Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing Services
- Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon termination

### 6.3 Duration
This confidentiality obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement and continue for a period of **two (2) years** thereafter.

## 7. NON-SOLICITATION

During the term of this Agreement and for **one (1) year** following termination, the Contractor shall not:

- Directly or indirectly contact, solicit, or conduct business with any property owners or leads obtained through the Company

- Solicit or recruit any of the Company's employees or contractors
