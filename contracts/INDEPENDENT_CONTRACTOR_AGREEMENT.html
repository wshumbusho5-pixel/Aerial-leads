1. ENGAGEMENT

The Company hereby engages the Contractor, and the Contractor agrees to provide services to the Company as an Independent Contractor in the role of:

Property Acquisition Specialist (PAS)
Investor Development Specialist (IDS)
Other:

2. SERVICES

The Contractor agrees to perform the following services ("Services"):

For Property Acquisition Specialist (PAS):

  • Contact property owners via phone regarding potential property sales
  • Follow Company-provided scripts and communication guidelines
  • Log all calls and outcomes in the Company's CRM system (AERIAL)
  • Set appointments for the acquisitions team
  • Submit daily performance reports
  • Maintain professional communication standards

For Investor Development Specialist (IDS):

  • Research and identify potential real estate investors
  • Conduct outreach via phone and email to build the buyer network
  • Collect and document investor criteria and contact information
  • Maintain accurate records in the Company's database
  • Submit daily performance reports
  • Follow up with interested investors as directed

3. COMPENSATION

3.1 Monthly Salary

The Contractor shall receive a fixed monthly salary of:

UGX 500,000 (Ugandan Shillings) per month

3.2 Payment Schedule

Salary shall be paid monthly on the 1st of each month for the previous month's work.

3.3 Payment Method

Payments shall be made via:

Mobile Money (MTN, Airtel)
Bank Transfer
PayPal
Wise (TransferWise)
Other:

3.4 Performance Bonuses

The Company may, at its sole discretion, award monthly performance bonuses to top-performing Contractors. Eligibility is based on:

  • Overall performance metrics (calls made, appointments set, quality scores)
  • Attendance and reliability
  • Professionalism and attitude
  • Contribution to team goals

Top Performer Awards: Each month, the Company may recognize the top 2 performers across all teams with a discretionary bonus. Selection is made entirely by the Company and is not guaranteed.

Note: Performance bonuses are not an entitlement and remain at the Company's discretion.

4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

4.1 Relationship

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture.

4.2 No Employee Benefits

The Contractor is not entitled to any employee benefits including but not limited to: health insurance, retirement benefits, paid vacation or sick leave, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance.

4.3 Taxes

The Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes arising from compensation received under this Agreement, including but not limited to income taxes and self-employment taxes. The Company will not withhold any taxes from payments to the Contractor.

4.4 Direction and Oversight

The Contractor agrees to perform Services under the direction and oversight of the Company. The Company shall provide scripts, training, quality standards, and work schedules that the Contractor is expected to follow.

5. SCHEDULE AND AVAILABILITY

5.1 Work Hours

The Contractor agrees to work the following required schedule:

9:00 AM - 5:00 PM Eastern Time

5.2 Work Days

The Contractor shall work five (5) days per week with the following days off:

  • Sunday - Compulsory day off
  • One additional day - Selected by the Contractor and approved by the Company

The selected day off must be consistent each week unless otherwise approved by the Company.

Contractor's Selected Day Off:

5.3 Communication

The Contractor agrees to:

  • Respond to messages within 4 hours during working hours
  • Attend weekly team calls as scheduled

5.4 Absences

Any planned absence must be:

  • Requested at least 24 hours in advance
  • Approved in writing by the Company before the absence

Unapproved absences may affect continued engagement with the Company.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Confidential Information

The Contractor acknowledges that during the engagement, they will have access to confidential and proprietary information including but not limited to:

  • Lead data and contact information
  • Property owner information
  • Investor/buyer lists and criteria
  • Company scripts, training materials, and methodologies
  • The AERIAL platform and its features
  • Business strategies and processes
  • Financial information

6.2 Non-Disclosure

The Contractor agrees to:

  • Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
  • Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party
  • Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing Services
  • Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon termination

6.3 Duration

This confidentiality obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement and continue for a period of two (2) years thereafter.

7. NON-SOLICITATION

During the term of this Agreement and for one (1) year following termination, the Contractor shall not:

  • Directly or indirectly contact, solicit, or conduct business with any property owners or leads obtained through the Company
  • Directly or indirectly contact, solicit, or conduct business with any investors or buyers from the Company's database
  • Solicit or recruit any of the Company's employees or contractors

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 Company Property

The Contractor acknowledges that the following are and shall remain the exclusive property of the Company:

  • All lead data generated or accessed during the engagement
  • The AERIAL platform and all related technology
  • All scripts, training materials, and methodologies
  • All work product created during the engagement

8.2 No Rights Granted

This Agreement does not grant the Contractor any ownership or license rights to any Company intellectual property.

9. EQUIPMENT AND TOOLS

9.1 Contractor-Provided

The Contractor shall provide at their own expense:

  • Computer/laptop with reliable internet connection
  • Headset suitable for phone calls
  • Quiet workspace suitable for professional calls

9.2 Company-Provided

The Company shall provide:

  • Access to the AERIAL platform
  • Access to the VA Portal
  • Phone system/dialer access
  • Training materials and scripts

10. STANDARDS OF CONDUCT

The Contractor agrees to:

  • Maintain professional and respectful communication at all times
  • Follow all Company scripts and guidelines
  • Never make promises or guarantees to property owners or investors
  • Never provide legal, tax, or financial advice
  • Immediately remove anyone from contact lists upon request (DNC compliance)
  • Never falsify call logs, reports, or other records
  • Protect the Company's reputation at all times

11. TERM AND TERMINATION

11.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on (Start Date) and continue until terminated by either party.

11.2 Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement:

  • With 7 days written notice for any reason
  • Immediately for cause (breach of Agreement, misconduct, etc.)

11.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:

  • Contractor shall immediately cease all work
  • Contractor shall return or destroy all Company materials and data
  • Company shall pay for all Services rendered through the termination date
  • Confidentiality and non-solicitation obligations shall survive

12.2 Amendments: This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

12.3 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Ohio, United States.

12.4 Dispute Resolution: Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through mediation before any legal action is taken.

12.5 Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
