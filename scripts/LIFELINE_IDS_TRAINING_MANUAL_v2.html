SECTION 5

Psychology Principles for IDS

Investors are different from sellers. They're business-minded, time-conscious, and looking for opportunity. But psychology still matters.

1 Lead with Value

Investors get pitched constantly. Open with what you can offer them, not what you want from them.

Example: "We source off-market distressed properties in Columbus" tells them immediately what's in it for them.

2 Be Direct

Investors respect directness. Unlike sellers who need emotional connection, investors want you to get to the point.

Example: "Are you actively buying investment properties in Ohio?" - Clean, simple, respectful of their time.

3 Speak Their Language

Use investor terminology: ARV, cash flow, cap rate, BRRRR, buy box. It signals you're legitimate.

4 No-Oriented Questions

People find it easier to say "no" than "yes." Frame questions where "no" works in your favor.

  • "Would it be crazy to..." - "No" means they'll do it
  • "Would you be opposed to..." - "No" means yes
  • "Is there any reason you wouldn't..." - "No" means proceed

5 Reciprocity

Give before you ask. Offer value first (market insights, deal examples) before asking for their criteria.

6 Social Proof

Mention you work with other active investors in the area. Nobody wants to miss what others are getting.

SECTION 9

Qualifying Buyers

Focus your energy on the best buyers. Not all contacts are equal.

A-LIST Highest Priority

  • Actively buying now
  • Closed deals in past 6 months
  • Funding ready (cash or hard money)
  • Clear, specific criteria
  • Responds quickly to deals

B-LIST Solid

  • Interested with experience
  • Looking but hasn't bought recently
  • General criteria (needs refining)

SECTION 10

Handling Objections

"How did you find me?"
"I was doing some research on active investors in the Columbus market and came across your profile/posts. We work with a lot of buyers in the area."
"I get calls like this all the time."
"I'm sure you do - that's what happens when you're an active buyer. Here's the difference with us: we actually bring deals. Would it be worth seeing one example before you decide?"
"I already have wholesalers I work with."
"That's great - most serious buyers do. We're not looking to replace anyone. But having more deal flow never hurts, right? Would you be opposed to us sending you deals that match your criteria?"
"What kind of deals do you have?"
"Off-market distressed properties - tax delinquent, probate, code violations. Properties where sellers need to move fast. What's your buy box? I'll tell you if we have anything that fits."
"I'm not buying right now."
"No problem. Would it be crazy if I kept you in our system for when you're ready? I can send you a market update now and then - no pressure."
"Take me off your list."
"Done. You're removed. Appreciate your time."
Never argue. Never push. Investors respect professionalism. Exit gracefully and leave the door open.
