UNDERSTANDING INVESTOR PSYCHOLOGY

Investors are DIFFERENT from distressed sellers:

  • They're business-minded - They want numbers, not emotions
  • They're skeptical - They've heard every pitch
  • They value time - Don't waste it with fluff
  • They want deals - That's the bottom line
  • They have criteria - Know them or you're wasting everyone's time

Your goal: Get them on the buyers list, understand their criteria, and build a relationship so when you have a deal - they're the first call.

SCRIPT 1: INITIAL INVESTOR OUTREACH

Best for: First contact with potential buyers from your investor leads list

THEM: Hello?

YOU: Hey [FIRST NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with Lifeline Home Buyers. I'll cut right to it - I see you've been buying properties in [COLUMBUS/CINCINNATI] area. I'm a wholesaler and I'm putting together my serious buyers list. Got 30 seconds?

THEM: Okay, go ahead.

YOU: Cool. So here's the deal - we source off-market properties before they hit MLS. Distressed, estate sales, pre-foreclosure situations. Real deals, not retail.

I'm calling to see two things: one, if you're still actively buying, and two, what your criteria is so I can actually send you deals that make sense. What are you looking for right now?

IF THEY GIVE CRITERIA

YOU: Got it. So you're looking for [REPEAT CRITERIA - property type, area, price range, ARV requirements].

What's your typical purchase range? And do you prefer cash or do you use financing?

FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS TO ASK

  • "What areas are you focused on? Any zip codes you absolutely won't touch?"
  • "What's your typical rehab budget range?"
  • "Are you looking for flips only, or buy-and-hold too?"
  • "How fast can you typically close when you see a deal you like?"
  • "What does a deal need to look like for you to move on it same-day?"

IF THEY SAY: "Just send me what you have"

YOU: I could do that, but honestly - I'd rather send you one deal that actually fits than blast you with ten that don't. Takes 2 minutes. What areas are you buying in right now?

CLOSING THE CALL

YOU: Perfect. So just to confirm - you're looking for [CRITERIA], in [AREAS], [PRICE RANGE], and you can close [TIMEFRAME].

I'll save your info. When I get something that fits, I'll shoot it over. Fair warning though - the good ones go fast, so be ready to move. What's the best email for me to send you deals?

SCRIPT 2: DEAL NOTIFICATION CALL

Best for: When you have an actual deal and need to move it

THEM: Hello?

YOU: Hey [FIRST NAME], it's [YOUR NAME] from Lifeline. Got a minute? I just picked up something I think fits your criteria.

THEM: What do you have?

YOU: Alright, so it's a [PROPERTY TYPE] over on [STREET/AREA]. [BEDS/BATHS], about [SQFT] square feet. Needs [BRIEF REHAB DESCRIPTION].

Here's the numbers: We're asking [ASKING PRICE]. Comps in the area are running [ARV]. With a [ESTIMATED REHAB] rehab, you're looking at solid margin.

You want me to send you the details?

IF THEY WANT MORE INFO

YOU: Cool. What's the best email? I'll send you the address, pictures, and a full breakdown. Just a heads up - I've got a couple other buyers looking at this, so if you're interested, let me know today.

IF THEY WANT TO NEGOTIATE

YOU: Look, I hear you. Here's the thing - this is already priced for a quick close. There's margin in there for you. If the numbers work, they work. If they don't, no hard feelings.

What would you need the number to be for you to move on it today?

SCRIPT 3: CASH BUYER QUALIFICATION

Best for: Thoroughly vetting a potential buyer

Questions to ask:

  1. "How long have you been investing? How many deals have you done in the last year?" (Tells you experience level)
  2. "When you find a deal you like, how quickly can you actually close?" (Tells you if they're serious)
  3. "Are you buying with cash, or do you have financing lined up?" (Cash buyers move faster)
  4. "Do you have proof of funds you can send over? I usually ask for this before I send addresses." (Separates real buyers from tire-kickers)
  5. "What's the last deal you closed? What was the address?" (Verify they're legit)
  6. "If I sent you a deal right now that fit your criteria, could you get me an answer by end of day?" (Tests responsiveness)

RED FLAGS TO WATCH FOR

  • They can't provide proof of funds
  • They've never actually closed a deal
  • They want to "partner" or "bring in other people"
  • They take days to respond
  • They want deep discounts on already-discounted deals

GREEN FLAGS

  • Quick responses
  • Clear criteria with specific numbers
  • Proof of funds readily available
  • History of closed deals
  • They ask smart questions about the property

SCRIPT 4: FOLLOW-UP / RELATIONSHIP BUILDER

Best for: Staying top of mind with your buyer pool

THEM: Hello?

YOU: Hey [FIRST NAME], it's [YOUR NAME] with Lifeline. Just checking in - wanted to see how things are going on your end.

THEM: Good, just busy.

YOU: I hear you. Real quick - are you still buying? I'm getting ready to bring some new inventory soon and I want to make sure I've got my A-list updated.

THEM: Yeah, still looking.

YOU: Cool. Any changes to what you're looking for? Same areas, same price range?

(Get updated criteria if anything changed)

YOU: Perfect. I'll keep you posted. When a deal comes through that fits, you'll be one of my first calls. Talk soon.

SCRIPT 5: NETWORKING / REIA EVENTS

Best for: Meeting investors in person

YOU: Hey, I'm [YOUR NAME] with Lifeline Home Buyers. You're an investor, right?

THEM: Yeah.

YOU: Cool. We wholesale off-market deals in Columbus and Cincinnati. What kind of properties are you typically looking for?

(Get their criteria)

YOU: Got it. What's the best way to send you deals when we get something that fits? Email or text?

(Get contact info)

YOU: Perfect. I'll add you to my list. When I get something in your wheelhouse, I'll shoot it over. If it works, great. If not, no worries. Sound good?

SCRIPT 6: VOICEMAILS FOR INVESTORS

Max 15-20 seconds - Investors are busy, respect their time

VERSION 1 - NEW INVESTOR

"Hey [FIRST NAME], it's [YOUR NAME] with Lifeline Home Buyers. I'm a wholesaler in [COLUMBUS/CINCINNATI] and I'm putting together my buyers list. Looking for serious investors. If that's you, call me back - [NUMBER]. Again, [YOUR NAME], [NUMBER]."

VERSION 2 - HAVE A DEAL

"Hey [FIRST NAME], [YOUR NAME] here. Got a property I think fits your criteria - [AREA], [PROPERTY TYPE], priced to move. Call me back if you want details. [NUMBER]."

VERSION 3 - FOLLOW UP

"[FIRST NAME], it's [YOUR NAME] from Lifeline. Following up on the deal I sent over. Let me know if you're interested - I've got other buyers looking at it. [NUMBER]."

INVESTOR OBJECTION RESPONSES

"Just email me the deal."
Response: "I can, but I'd rather give you a heads up when something hot comes in - email can get buried. What's the best number to text you? I'll only hit you up when I have something that actually fits what you're looking for."
"Your price is too high."
Response: "I hear you. Where would you need to be for this to make sense? Let me see if there's any room."

Note: Don't give away margin too easily. Know your floor.
"I need to run the numbers."
Response: "Totally get it. Just know - this one's going to move fast. I've got a few other buyers looking. Can you get me an answer by [SPECIFIC TIME]?"
"I want to see it first."
Response: "Absolutely. When can you get there? I can get you access [TODAY/TOMORROW]. And just to be clear - if the numbers work for you, can you make a decision same-day?"
"Can you send me the address?"
Response: "I'll send you the full package - address, photos, numbers. Just need your email. And do you have proof of funds on file with me yet? I usually need that before sharing addresses."
"I only buy at X% ARV."
Response: "Got it. I'll note that in your profile. This one might not fit, but when something does hit that number, I'll send it straight to you."

BUILDING YOUR BUYERS LIST

TIER YOUR BUYERS

TIER 1 - A-List (The Closers)

  • They've bought from you before
  • They close fast with cash
  • They're responsive
  • They don't haggle on already-discounted deals

TIER 2 - B-List (The Potentials)

  • They have proof of funds
  • They're active but haven't bought from you yet
  • They have clear criteria
  • They take a bit longer to respond

TIER 3 - C-List (The Maybe)

  • They're interested but no deals closed
  • They don't have proof of funds yet
  • They're new to investing
  • They're "looking" but not moving

Priority: Always call Tier 1 first. Then Tier 2. Tier 3 gets email blasts.

WHAT TO TRACK FOR EACH BUYER

# Information to Track
1Name & contact info
2Areas they buy in (specific zip codes)
3Property types (SFH, multi, land)
4Price range (min and max)
5Rehab level they can handle (light, medium, heavy)
6Closing speed (days or weeks)
7Cash or financing
8Last deal they closed (verify they're real)
9Responsiveness rating (how fast they reply)
10Notes from conversations

GOLDEN RULES FOR INVESTOR CALLS

  1. Respect their time - Get to the point fast
  2. Know your numbers - They will ask
  3. Create urgency (honestly) - Good deals do go fast
  4. Qualify hard - Better 10 real buyers than 100 flakes
  5. Follow up - Relationships are built over time
  6. Deliver on promises - If you say you'll send the deal, send it immediately
  7. Don't oversell - If it's a deal, the numbers speak for themselves
  8. Build the list every day - Your buyers list is your distribution network
  9. Move fast - When you have a deal, call within hours, not days
  10. Know when to walk away - If a buyer is difficult, focus on others
