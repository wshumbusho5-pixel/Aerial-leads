PART 3: THE PSYCHOLOGY OF COLD CALLING

Before you learn the scripts, you must understand WHY they work. Memorizing words without understanding the psychology will make you sound robotic. Understanding the psychology will make you sound natural.

PRINCIPLE 1: THE 7-SECOND RULE

What it is: People decide if you're worth listening to within 7 seconds of answering.

What this means: Your opening line is EVERYTHING. Sound like a typical salesperson = they hang up mentally.

How to apply: Sound casual, not corporate. Create curiosity immediately. Never start with a pitch.

PRINCIPLE 2: CURIOSITY IS AN ITCH

What it is: When there's a gap between what someone knows and what they WANT to know, they feel an uncomfortable itch that must be scratched.

How to apply: Don't give information freely. Create gaps. Make them WANT to ask you questions.

  • BAD: "I want to buy your house for cash"
  • GOOD: "Your property came up for a specific reason..."

PRINCIPLE 3: "HEY [NAME]" NOT "IS THIS [NAME]?"

What it is: Friends don't ask "Is this John?" - they just start talking. Asking signals you're a stranger.

DON'T SAY DO SAY
"Hello, is this John Smith?" "Hey John, this is Mike with Lifeline..."

PRINCIPLE 4: QUESTIONS BEAT STATEMENTS

What it is: Statements trigger skepticism. Questions trigger imagination.

  • STATEMENT: "We buy houses fast for cash" → They think "Yeah, sure"
  • QUESTION: "What would it be like if you could skip all the traditional stuff - no repairs, no showings, no waiting months?" → They imagine it

PRINCIPLE 5: NO-ORIENTED QUESTIONS

What it is: People find it easier to say "No" than "Yes." Use this to your advantage.

Format Example Why It Works
"Would it be crazy to..." "Would it be crazy to explore your options?" "No, not crazy" = They're open
"I'm guessing you probably haven't..." "I'm guessing you probably haven't thought about selling... or have you?" "No, actually I have" = They open up

PRINCIPLE 6: NEVER MENTION SHAME DIRECTLY

What it is: Tax delinquency, foreclosure, code violations carry shame. Calling it out = they shut down.

DON'T: "I see you're behind on property taxes..."

DO: "Your property came up in some research I was doing on properties that might need some attention..."

PRINCIPLE 7: VOICE TONE MATTERS MORE THAN WORDS

Research shows 38% of communication is tone, only 7% is words.

Tone When to Use How It Sounds
Positive/Playful Opening the call Light, warm, casual
Late Night FM DJ Handling objections Slow, calm, deep
Direct/Assertive Important points Neutral, matter-of-fact

Rules: Slow down. Smile while talking. Pause after important points.

PRINCIPLE 8: MIRROR THEIR WORDS

Repeat the last 1-3 words as a question. They elaborate and feel heard.

THEM: "I'm not really interested right now."
YOU: "Not interested right now?" (curious tone)
THEM: "Well, I mean... we've thought about it, but the timing isn't right..."

PART 4: THE SCRIPTS

MAIN OPENER - THE CURIOSITY APPROACH

Use for: Outbound calls to AERIAL leads

THEM: Hello?

YOU: Hey [FIRST NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with Lifeline - I hope I caught you at an okay time.

[PAUSE - wait for response]

YOU: I've got kind of an unusual question for you...

[PAUSE - let curiosity build]

THEM: Okay...?

YOU: So I was looking through some properties in [AREA], and yours came up for a specific reason - and honestly, I'm not even sure if I can help you, but I had to at least call and find out.

THEM: What do you mean? / Help with what?

YOU: I work with homeowners who are looking at their options - people who might be thinking about moving on from a property but don't want to deal with the traditional process.

[PAUSE - let them respond]

WHEN THEY ENGAGE: "What kind of help?"

YOU: So let me ask you something - and just think about this for a second...

What would it be like for you if you could skip all the traditional stuff? No having to list it, no spending money on repairs, no strangers walking through your home judging everything, no waiting months wondering if it's actually going to close...

[PAUSE - let them imagine]

What would that kind of simplicity mean for your situation right now?

NO-ORIENTED QUESTIONS TO USE

Format 1: "Would it be crazy to at least explore what your options might be - even if you decide it's not for you?"

Format 2: "I'm guessing you probably haven't thought about doing anything with the property... or have you?"

SETTING THE APPOINTMENT (VIRTUAL FIRST)

YOU: Here's what I'm thinking - and you can totally say no -

Would it be crazy if we hopped on a quick call to chat more about your situation? Just 10-15 minutes, no pressure, no commitment. I just want to learn a bit more and see if we might be able to help.

What works better for you - sometime this week or next?

THEM: [Picks a timeframe]

YOU: Perfect. Morning or afternoon?

THEM: [Picks a time]

YOU: Great. What's the best email to send you a calendar invite? That way you'll have all the details and a reminder.
Why Virtual First? Lower commitment = easier "yes." Qualifies the lead and builds rapport before anyone visits in person.

INBOUND WEBSITE LEADS

Call within 5 minutes. These are HOT. Speed matters.

YOU: Hey [FIRST NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with Lifeline Home Buyers. I saw you submitted an inquiry about [ADDRESS].

Tell me about your situation - what made you reach out?

[Let them talk. Mirror. Label emotions. Take notes.]

CALLBACK QUEUE

YOU: Hey [FIRST NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] with Lifeline Home Buyers. Thanks for calling us back.

You received a message about your property on [ADDRESS]. I'm guessing you probably wouldn't have called back if you weren't at least a little curious... what's going on with the property?

PART 5: HANDLING OBJECTIONS

THE RULE: Never argue. Never pressure. Validate first, then ask a question.

"I'm not interested."

YOU: Totally understand.

[PAUSE]

Can I ask you one quick thing? Just so I know whether to follow up later or take you off my list completely - is it more that you love the property and can't see yourself leaving? Or is it more about timing right now?

"How did you get my number?"

YOU: Fair question. Your property came up in some research I was doing on [AREA] - I work with a local company called Lifeline Home Buyers, and we reach out to homeowners to see if we might be able to help. Most people tell me no, and that's completely fine. I just didn't want to miss someone who might actually be looking for a simpler way forward.

"How much will you offer?"

YOU: Fair question. I can't throw out a number without knowing more about the place - that wouldn't be fair to either of us. But what I can tell you is we're fair, we're fast, and we don't play games.

Let me ask you - what would you need to get out of it for a sale to make sense?

"I've talked to investors before - they're all lowballers."

YOU: I hear that a lot, and honestly, some of them are.

[PAUSE]

Here's the difference with us - we're not looking to steal anything. We're looking for situations where cash and speed have real value. If you need top retail dollar, we're probably not the right fit. But if you need simple and certain... that's exactly what we do.

"Take me off your list."

YOU: Done. You're removed. Have a good day, [NAME].

PART 6: SPECIAL SITUATIONS

1. GATEKEEPER (LLCs / Companies)

THEM: "[COMPANY NAME], how can I help you?"

YOU: Hey, this is [YOUR NAME] - I had a quick question about the property on [ADDRESS]. Who handles those decisions?

THEM: What's this regarding?

YOU: We've been looking at a few properties in that area and that one came up. Just wanted to have a quick conversation with whoever manages it - is that something you handle, or is there someone else I should talk to?

2. WRONG PERSON (But Might Know Owner)

YOU: Hey, I'm trying to reach [OWNER NAME] about a property on [ADDRESS]. I don't suppose you'd know how I could get in touch with them?

[If they know the owner]

YOU: Oh perfect. Would you mind passing along my info? Just tell them [YOUR NAME] from Lifeline called about the property - they can reach me at [NUMBER]. I really appreciate you helping me out.

3. ANGRY ANSWER

THEM: "WHAT DO YOU WANT?!" [Hostile tone]

YOU: (Slow down. Late Night FM DJ voice. Stay completely calm.)

Hey... sounds like I caught you at a rough time. I can call back - would another day work better?

[If they stay hostile:]

YOU: No problem at all. I'll let you go. Take care.
NEVER argue. NEVER explain. NEVER match their energy. Your calm is your power.

4. CLOSING CONFIRMATION

YOU: Perfect. So just to confirm - we'll give you a call [DAY] around [TIME] to chat more about your situation. I'll send you a calendar invite so you have it.

If anything changes on your end, just give us a call. Otherwise, we'll talk to you [DAY].

Appreciate your time, [NAME].

5. SOFT EXIT (Dead Lead)

YOU: I hear you - totally understand.

I'll take you off our list. If anything ever changes down the road, you've got our number.

Take care, [NAME].
